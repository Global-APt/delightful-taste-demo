# Publish the demo with GitHub Pages

1. Open https://github.com/Global-APt/delightful-taste-demo/settings/pages
2. Under Build and deployment, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Branch: `main`; folder: `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Wait for the Pages build to finish, then open https://global-apt.github.io/delightful-taste-demo/.

The demo is a public prototype using sample data. Orders and sales are only stored in the current browser session; they are not synced between devices. Payments are simulated. Do not enter real customer details or payment information.
